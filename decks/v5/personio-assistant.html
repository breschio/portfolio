Assistant

How I helped evolve the AI Assistant from a broken UX to a full-blown agentic strategy.

Project Personio AI Team
Role Product Designer
Timeline May 2025 — Current
Status 🔒 case study deck
personio assistant · what it is 04 / 33

“The Intelligent HR Platform”

We aim to leverage AI to help our customers gain efficiencies and lower costs.

— attractive and hand-wavy at the same time.

customer problems 05 / 33

Overextended HR
professionals.

HR admins, managers and employees at European tech companies. These folks had high expectations — and real pain.

  1. 01Drowning in questions from employees
  2. 02Managing employee job changes
  3. 03Juggling roles between admin and strategy
  4. 04Excited yet skeptical appetites for AI
solution 06 / 33

The Assistant.

An AI assistant built to provide insights while deflecting common questions.

  1. 01Deflect support tickets from employees
  2. 02Understand your company data
  3. 03Produce insights and visualizations
  4. 04Empower you to become a strategic partner
problem 07 / 33

CSAT was at 16%.

We have some fundamental issues surfaced in Dogfooding.

  1. 01Issues with data accuracy impact trust
  2. 02Issues with UX impact usability + look and feel
  3. 03These projects were not on the roadmap
problems identified Original Figma Files. Designs were aspirational but one-dimensional — UX had not been scoped. 08 / 33
process 09 / 33

How I fix problems.

I like to prototype on production.

  1. 01Move fast with smaller scope
  2. 02Build functionality iteratively
  3. 03Test the UX and share
  4. 04Identify issues I wouldn't catch otherwise
  5. 05Then refine with Design Systems
