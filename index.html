Drawbridge

2025 / Cursor, Figma

Case study → Github

A Chrome plugin I built for AI-driven design and development. It allows you to make ‘Figma comments’ in the browser for processing by Claude or Cursor.

Open source. 900+ stars on GitHub. The case study unpacks the build story — how the annotation primitive came from a hallway gripe and turned into an open tool.

Pizza Cat

2024 / Cursor, Figma, Supabase, Google Music FX

Play Github

A simple game I built to learn the fundamentals of AI-driven development. I wanted to understand how AI can help create interactive experiences from scratch.

Soundtrack scored with Google Music FX. Backend on Supabase. Built end-to-end with Cursor — first time I shipped a real game.

Meta Avatars Store

2020 / Figma

I designed the shopping experience for Meta’s Avatar Store, making digital self-expression feel personal through clothing and accessories.

Worked across Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger surfaces. The challenge: a shopping flow that felt native to each app while sharing one inventory model.

Instagram Action Buttons

2020 / Figma

I streamlined the setup for businesses to get bookings, leads, and sales directly from their Instagram profiles.

A small surface — one button on a profile — with big consequences for SMB conversion. The hard part was the setup flow: connecting business tools without making it feel like an enterprise install.

Facebook Ads Design System

2019 / Figma

I developed and tested a design system that unified the advertising experience across Facebook, Instagram, and Business Suite — reducing inconsistencies and improving design velocity.

One source of truth for hundreds of components shipping to millions of advertisers. The hard part wasn’t the tokens — it was getting four product teams to agree on what ‘Ad’ meant.
