Animated ASCII scenes for your terminal — a lava lamp you run with
`javalamp`. It holds `caffeinate -d -i` open while it plays, so the
screensaver is also the reason your Mac doesn't fall asleep mid-demo.

The idea took an afternoon. The interesting part was deciding to treat it
like a real package anyway: tests, CI, a changelog, a contributor guide,
issue templates, a documented format for adding your own scenes, an MIT
license, and a publish workflow.

That scaffolding is exactly the work that used to keep small ideas from
becoming shipped things — too much ceremony for a joke about a lava lamp.
With AI carrying most of it, the calculus changes. The question stops being
"is this worth the setup" and becomes "is this worth an afternoon."

More things should be worth an afternoon.
